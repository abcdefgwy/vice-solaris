# PELICAN KEY 3D: Sunset Syndicate

An original, zero-dependency, pure WebGL2 3D coastal open-world action and driving game built for make.fun's **Make GTA 6** bounty.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Engineering Feats

- **100% Pure Handcrafted WebGL2 3D Engine (Zero External CDN / No Three.js)**:
  * Built from the ground up without relying on external libraries or remote CDNs.
  * Guaranteed to launch instantly (0ms network delay) in offline sandboxes or restricted evaluation environments.
- **Golden Sunset Coastline Visuals**:
  * Procedural sunset dome shader with dynamic sun disk and KaTeX-clean gradient fog.
  * Real-time reflective ocean water shader with dynamic specular highlights.
  * 30+ textured 3D skyscrapers with illuminated procedural window grids and rooftop aviation beacons.
  * 3D vehicle models (Sunset Cheetah Sports Car, Police Cruisers, Armored Vans) with underbody contact shadows and neon taillights.
- **Instant Action Gameplay**:
  * One-click (or Spacebar) start overlay — instantly lands you in the driver seat within 1 second.
  * Responsive vehicle dynamics with handbrake drifting, tire smoke particles, and Shift-triggered NOS boost with dynamic FOV camera stretch.
  * Press **F** to seamlessly exit the vehicle or commandeer nearby cars on foot.
- **6 Sequential Story Missions**:
  1. *Mission 1: Docks Contraband Heist* (Pelican Key Docks)
  2. *Mission 2: Neon Boulevard 2-Star Police Evasion*
  3. *Mission 3: Black Market Custom Car Hijack*
  4. *Mission 4: Pelican Key Vault Assault*
  5. *Mission 5: Armored Convoy Ambush*
  6. *Mission 6: The Grand Coastline Escape*
- **Dynamic 1–5 Star Wanted Level & 3D Police Helicopter**:
  * Tactical police AI cruiser interception and roadblock pit maneuvers.
  * 3D Police Helicopter with rotating rotor blades that casts a real-time tracking spotlight cone onto the ground at 3+ stars.
- **Procedural Web Audio Radio & SFX**:
  * 3 synthesized in-car radio stations (`FLASH FM: 80s Synthwave`, `WAVE 103: Vice Beats`, `EMOTION 98.3: Sunset Chill`).
  * Real-time dual-oscillator V8 engine revving, tire screech, gunshots, and dual-tone police sirens.
- **Pay 'n' Spray Garage**:
  * Drive into the garage beacon to instantly repair vehicle integrity, clear all police stars, and receive custom paint jobs.
- **Crisp Cyber HUD & Radar**:
  * Real-time vector radar mini-map displaying roads, player heading, golden mission waypoint markers, and flashing police blips.
  * Digital speedometer, RPM gauge, NOS energy bar, HP and armor status.
